Timeline-First Investigation

Workflow

From first alert to final report,
without the chaos.

IRDoc gives every analyst the same structured, repeatable workflow — whether it's a first-year analyst or a senior IR engineer.

🎫
Case opens automatically
SDP, ManageEngine, or Jira pushes the ticket to IRDoc via one API call. Task checklist loads instantly from the incident template.
📋
Document as you investigate
Add timeline entries, paste screenshots, auto-detect IOCs in your notes. Real-time sync means your whole team sees updates as they happen.
🎯
IOCs enrich automatically
Every indicator is checked against VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB the moment it's added. Confidence scores recalculate. AI writes the plain-English narrative.
📤
Management is always informed
SharePoint auto-sync regenerates the management brief 60 seconds after every change. They open a link — no reports to request, no stale documents.

SharePoint Auto-Sync

Management is always
up to date

Every time the incident is updated — new timeline entry, task ticked, IOC added — the management document on SharePoint regenerates automatically. No manual report generation. No stale documents. Management opens a link.

⏱
60-second debounce
Rapid changes don't fire multiple uploads. IRDoc waits 60 seconds of silence after the last change, then renders and uploads once.
🎨
Uses your Management template
The sync uses whichever report template you assign — customised for non-technical readers. No technical jargon in the management brief.
🔐
They never need IRDoc access
Management reads a SharePoint document — the same tool they already use. No new logins, no complex UI, no training required.
